## About the Movie
"Your Name" is a 2016 Japanese animated film directed by Makoto Shinkai. It tells the story of two teenagers, Mitsuha and Taki, who begin to mysteriously swap bodies and develop a powerful connection across time and distance.

> "I hate this town! I hate this life! Please make me a handsome Tokyo boy in my next life!" - My Favorite qoute in the series 🤣🤣🤣

- Original title: Kimi no Na wa
- Director: Makoto Shinkai
- Release year: 2016
- Genre: Romance, Drama, Fantasy, Animation

## Why I Love It
1. Beautiful animation and the art that was Magestic and Beautiful.
2. Emotional storytelling with unexpected twists.
3. A memorable soundtrack that makes every scene so much impactful.

## Main Characters
- Mitsuha Miyamizu - a high-school girl from a rural town.
- Taki Tachibana - a high-school boy living in Tokyo.
- Supporting cast: friends and family who shape the story.

## Themes & Motifs
- Fate vs. free will
- Memory and identity
- The red thread / connections between people
- Time, loss, and hope

## Favorite Scenes
- The first body-swap scene - it was super funny and unexpected when i watched it for the first time.
- The comet sequence - the visuals are super stunning and very emotional.
- The final attempts to meet - super tense and heartfelt(i cried).
